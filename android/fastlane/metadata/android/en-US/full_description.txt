GAlMax is an open and feature-rich Matrix messenger app for Android and iOS.

Open
Open source and open development where everyone can join.

Feature-rich
GAlMax comes with GIF integration, round video messages, proximity playback, and more.

Cute
Cute design and many theme settings including a dark mode and custom accent colors.

One-to-one and group chats
Unlimited groups and direct chats.

Easy
GAlMax is made as simple to use as possible.

Free
Free to use for everyone without ads.

Decentralized
There is no "GAlMax server" you are forced to use. Use the server you find trustworthy or host your own.

Compatible
Compatible with Element, FluffyChat, Nheko and all Matrix messengers.
